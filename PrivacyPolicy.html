Privacy Policy for VTPlayX

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VTPlayX, developed by Vishal, handles information when you use the VTPlayX mobile application.

1. About VTPlayX

VTPlayX is a mobile game application that provides multiple small games within one application.

2. Information We Collect

VTPlayX does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password to use the app.

Based on the current version of the app, we do not intentionally collect, store, or transmit personal information to our own servers.

3. Device and Game Data

The app may use information that is necessary for normal operation of the Android application and its games, such as locally stored game settings or game progress, where applicable. Such information remains on the user's device unless the app specifically provides a feature that transfers it.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of VTPlayX does not use Firebase, advertising SDKs, analytics services, or other third-party services intended to collect personal information.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data collection, use, and sharing before or when those services are introduced.

5. Advertising

VTPlayX currently does not display advertisements and does not currently use an advertising SDK.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated to accurately describe the advertising service and any data it may process.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties because VTPlayX does not currently collect personal information through its own services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. Since VTPlayX does not currently maintain user accounts or transmit personal information to our own servers, there is no user account database maintained by us.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not retain personal information on our own servers because VTPlayX does not currently collect or maintain such information.

If the app stores local game settings or progress on your device, you can generally remove that local data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to Android device behavior.

9. Children's Privacy

VTPlayX does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at the same privacy-policy webpage, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
