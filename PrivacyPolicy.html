2. Information We Collect and Store

VTPlayX does not require users to create an online account or log in to use the application.

However, the application may store certain information locally on the user's Android device using the device's local storage, including AsyncStorage. The information stored locally depends on the features used by the user.

This may include information such as user preferences, game settings, game progress, selected options, locally saved user information, and other information required to provide and maintain app functionality.

The locally stored information is used only to provide app functionality, maintain user preferences, and preserve relevant information between app sessions.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored locally on your device may be used to:

  • Maintain your app and game preferences.
  • Save game progress or settings where applicable.
  • Remember information entered or selected within the app.
  • Provide and maintain the functionality of VTPlayX.

VTPlayX does not use locally stored information for advertising, profiling, or selling user information.

4. Local Storage and AsyncStorage

VTPlayX uses Android local storage, including AsyncStorage, to store certain app-related information on the user's device.

This information remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to our servers unless a specific feature of the application requires such transmission.

AsyncStorage is used as application storage and should not be considered a secure or encrypted storage mechanism for highly sensitive information. VTPlayX does not intentionally store passwords, financial information, or other highly sensitive information in AsyncStorage.

5. Data Sharing

VTPlayX does not sell, rent, or share users' locally stored information with third parties.

VTPlayX does not currently transmit locally stored user information to our own servers, except where a specific app feature requires such transmission.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the information collected and stored by VTPlayX. Information stored through AsyncStorage remains within the application's local storage on the user's device.

Users should avoid entering highly sensitive information into the app, such as passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information may remain on the user's device until it is removed by the application, overwritten, the app's storage is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Users can generally delete locally stored VTPlayX data by clearing the application's data through Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.
